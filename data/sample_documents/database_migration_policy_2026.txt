Enterprise Database Architecture Update (Release 3.1)
Date: 2026-01-10
Author: Enterprise Architecture Board
Topic: Database & Storage

PostgreSQL 16 is the mandatory enterprise database standard. MySQL 5.7 has reached end-of-life and is officially deprecated. All engineering teams must migrate active workloads to PostgreSQL 16 by Q2 2026. New transactional services are forbidden from provisioning MySQL instances.
